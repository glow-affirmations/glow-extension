# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project.

Participants should be considerate, assume good faith, give actionable
feedback, respect privacy, and focus disagreement on ideas and behavior.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or
other participation that violates these expectations, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[support@justglow.dev](mailto:support@justglow.dev). Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical. Good-faith retaliation is prohibited.
